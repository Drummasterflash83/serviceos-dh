ServiceOS pilot agreement for Drummonds

Approval draft • 13 October 2026 • Not signed or effective

This agreement covers the initial 90-day ServiceOS pilot for Drummonds. Chris has approved the proposed pilot duration, fee basis, cost approval, notice period and preservation of existing software ownership. Signature adopts the final agreed text; this draft does not authorise an unverified feature or change data retention by itself.

1. Parties and start

The parties are Chris Drummond, supplying and supporting OpenFolk ServiceOS personally, and DRUMMOND HEATING LTD., company number 07556814, of Unit 15 Westlink Belbins Business Park, Cupernham Lane, Romsey, Hampshire, England, SO51 7JF. OpenFolk ServiceOS is the product name, not a separate contracting company under this agreement.

Chris's notice address is chris@drummondheating.co.uk. Drummonds' nominated signatory is Heidi Drummond. Drummonds must nominate an email for Heidi's contractual notices when signing; the current application support address remains Chris's address above.

The pilot starts on the date of the last signature and runs for 90 days. Any extension requires written agreement; there is no automatic paid renewal.

2. What the pilot provides

Chris will provide a non-exclusive right for Drummonds' authorised users to use its ServiceOS workspace during the pilot, subject to the rights Chris is entitled to grant. He will develop, configure and support the enabled features with reasonable care and skill. Existing employment, shareholder or other agreements remain in force unless expressly varied in writing.

The pilot brings together authorised customer, job and communication information and, once authorised and verified, accounting information. A recorded release checklist identifies which features are operational, provisional or unavailable. Drummonds' original business systems remain its records of authority.

QuickBooks scope initially covers read-only customer, invoice, payment and credit-note records. Intuit grants a broader Accounting permission, but this connector must not create, change or delete accounting records or initiate payments. Adding bills, estimates, reports, payroll, bank feeds or financial writes requires a separate scope decision and verification.

Individual and group Slack DMs are excluded. AI receptionist activation, payment decisions and automatic changes to employee pay are not authorised by this agreement.

3. Fees and costs

There is no separate software fee during this pilot. Third-party charges require Drummonds' prior written approval of the service, budget and payer. This agreement does not expand any existing spending approval or let Chris incur unapproved charges on Drummonds' behalf. Any future paid service or extension must be agreed separately.

4. Responsibilities and access

Drummonds decides the business purposes, authorised users, connected accounts and import scope. It is responsible for appropriate notices and a lawful basis for its customer and workforce processing. Heidi signs on behalf of Drummonds and can nominate other authorised decision-makers in writing.

Chris is the nominated security-risk review owner. He will maintain an issue register, review material releases before launch, and conduct a recorded monthly review of access, vulnerabilities, recovery evidence and unresolved risks during the pilot. This is a proposed operating obligation on signature, not a claim that a review programme already existed. High-risk findings require containment and an agreed remediation or suspension decision.

OpenFolk support operators may access Drummonds' information only when authorised, confidential and necessary for support or the agreed implementation. Operator access must be logged and use explicit client selection. Ordinary client access remains isolated. An operator's access to several clients does not permit reuse of one client's source records for another.

5. Ownership and confidentiality

Existing ownership of ServiceOS, reusable software, third-party components and intellectual property remains unchanged. This agreement neither assigns Drummonds' existing rights to Chris nor assigns Chris's existing rights to Drummonds. Any uncertainty about background rights or newly commissioned work must be resolved in a written schedule before those rights are relied on. Drummonds retains its rights in its business records.

Each party will protect the other's non-public information, use it only for this agreement, and disclose it only to authorised people with corresponding confidentiality duties or as legally required. These duties continue after the pilot ends. No sale of Drummonds' data or reuse of its identifiable records for unrelated clients is permitted.

6. Data handling instructions

Drummonds is the controller for the operational purposes of this deployment. Chris will act under its documented instructions. Where his activities constitute processor services, the processing provisions below apply; the parties must confirm the actual role allocation before signature rather than relying on the product name.

The processing consists of collecting, securely storing, organising, displaying and supporting the correction/export of authorised business records, and generating agreed operational insights. It concerns customers, site occupants, contacts, suppliers, staff and correspondents. It can include identifiers, contact details, job details, accounting transactions, communications, recordings, transcripts and access records. It lasts for the pilot and the restricted exit/recovery period.

Chris must process personal data only on Drummonds' recorded instructions, including for international transfers, unless UK law requires otherwise. He must inform Drummonds of a conflicting legal requirement where permitted and flag instructions he reasonably believes unlawful. Everyone allowed to process the data must be bound to confidentiality.

Chris must apply appropriate access, encryption, credential, logging, recovery and vulnerability-management measures; assist Drummonds with rights requests, impact assessments and regulator consultation where required; and provide relevant compliance information and allow reasonable audits. An incident affecting Drummonds must be notified to its nominated representative without undue delay, with information and updates sufficient for Drummonds to assess its own notification duties. No guaranteed detection time or 24-hour support service is implied.

Additional processors require prior written authorisation and corresponding data-protection obligations. Chris remains responsible for any processors he engages for these services as required by applicable law. The supplier register must identify which contracts Drummonds holds directly and which Chris holds, the services, locations, transfer safeguards and change-notification arrangements. Approval of the software alone is not approval of an undisclosed supplier or international transfer.

QuickBooks data is excluded from model training and fine-tuning. Its current connector does not send accounting records into an AI processing pipeline. Any later inference or analytical AI use needs documented scope, provider/data-use review and the required approval; it must not weaken the training prohibition.

7. Retention and exit proposal

For express approval and operational verification before adoption: retain pilot records only where needed for the agreed use, review them at the 90-day pilot end, and retain longer only for a documented continuing purpose or legal hold. The original QuickBooks system remains the financial source of record; this pilot does not set statutory accounting retention.

On termination, stop new capture and revoke connector access as instructed. At Drummonds' choice, return its retained data in an available structured format or delete it, unless UK law requires retention. Proposed targets are an export opportunity of 30 days after termination, removal from active ServiceOS storage within 30 days after return/deletion is agreed, and removal from recovery copies within 90 days after active deletion. Copies awaiting deletion must be restricted to recovery/legal need and deletion instructions reapplied after a restore. Provide a completion record and explain any legal exception.

Those targets must be reconciled with all source versions, derived records and backup locks before signature. Existing backup protection is a minimum retention lock, and some current backups have no automatic expiry. This draft neither claims the targets are implemented nor authorises deletion of current data.

8. Support and limitations

Support is through Chris at chris@drummondheating.co.uk. The pilot carries no guaranteed uptime, response time, recovery time, revenue outcome or completeness promise. Chris must report material stale data, gaps and failures accurately and work with Drummonds to prioritise fixes. Planned work must not be presented as verified live operation.

Drummonds must check important findings against source records. AI outputs and matching may be wrong. No output is an accounting audit, tax advice or authority to make a significant employment decision without meaningful human review.

9. Ending the pilot and liability

Either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice. A party may suspend affected processing immediately where necessary to protect data or comply with law, explaining the reason and next steps when lawful. Material breach may be terminated if not remedied within 14 days of written notice, or immediately if it cannot be remedied. Confidentiality, data-return/deletion and accrued obligations survive termination.

No monetary liability cap or indemnity is agreed in this draft. This leaves the applicable legal position unchanged; it is not an assumption that a free pilot removes liability. Nothing excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded. Both parties should consider this allocation before signing.

England and Wales law applies and its courts have jurisdiction, subject to mandatory applicable law. The parties will first try to resolve disputes through Chris and Heidi. Changes require a written agreement by both parties. Intuit and other providers' terms continue to apply separately.

10. Signature and completion checklist

Before signing, confirm the role/supplier schedule, retention targets and achievable deletion procedure, software rights, Heidi's notice email, and this agreement's liability position. The public privacy notice and authorised-user terms must match the adopted arrangements.
