Drummonds — OpenFolk ServiceOS privacy notice

The proposed commitments below need approval and operational verification through the launch approval pack before publication. It does not describe Drummonds as the operator of OpenFolk for other clients or certify that planned controls are already operational.

2. What information comes into ServiceOS

Information depends on the enabled modules, source permissions and agreed import scope. It may include:

  • Customer, supplier and staff names, contact details, addresses and identifiers.
  • Customer accounts, sites, job records, appointments, progress and operational notes.
  • Emails and metadata; call details, recordings and transcripts; voicemails; authorised channel messages, replies and participant details.
  • Accounting contacts and transactions, including invoices, payments, credits, bills, estimates, balances and the references linking those records.
  • Account roles, support correspondence, access logs and technical diagnostics.
  • Derived summaries, linked timelines, extracted tasks and indicators based on the available records.

Sources include people who contact or work with us, our authorised users, and connected business systems such as Google Workspace, our phone provider, Slack, Commusoft and, once authorised, QuickBooks Online. The relevant configuration controls what is captured. Source retention, access restrictions and technical failures can limit completeness or freshness.

Our current Slack rollout excludes individual and group direct messages. A change to that scope would require a separate decision, appropriate access and notice. Not every feature listed in this draft is enabled or verified today.

Our authorised technical support personnel may diagnose issues and improve our implementation under our instructions.

4. QuickBooks Online

Once enabled, an authorised administrator connects our specific QuickBooks company through Intuit’s authorisation process. The connector uses access tokens rather than a user’s QuickBooks password. It reads the agreed accounting records to help understand invoices, balances, payments and their relationship to our work.

The initial ServiceOS connector is designed not to create, edit or delete accounting transactions or initiate payments. Intuit’s Accounting permission includes read and write capability; ServiceOS restricts its implementation to reads. The separate payment-processing API and payroll are outside this initial connection.

QuickBooks data is excluded from model training and fine-tuning. The current connector does not send QuickBooks records to an AI processing pipeline.

An authorised administrator can revoke access through Intuit’s connected-app controls or request disconnection through our ServiceOS support contact. Disconnection does not change our records in QuickBooks or automatically erase copies already retained in ServiceOS. Retention and deletion are handled separately.

Launch check: the sandbox read-only import is verified. Drummonds' live connection and the complete disconnect/reconnect lifecycle must be verified before this notice is published as describing an available live connection.

5. AI and performance analysis

Enabled AI or speech features may send relevant information to the disclosed providers to create summaries, transcripts or other requested outputs. Such outputs can be inaccurate, incomplete or linked to the wrong record. Important conclusions must be checked against the underlying evidence.

Performance indicators, if enabled, require an explanation of their measures, coverage, purpose and correction process. Our proposed policy requires meaningful human review before decisions about pay, employment or other significant outcomes. A score is not an automatic instruction to change someone’s pay or employment. Any such feature requires assessment and appropriate workforce information before use.

Proposed data-use restriction: identifiable workspace information will not be sold, used to train general-purpose models or reused for unrelated client datasets. Client-specific support and software improvement remain within the agreed purposes. Verify and adopt this commitment in supplier contracts and configuration before publication; this draft does not claim that every AI provider offers zero retention.

7. Security and international processing

The service uses access restrictions, protected server-side credentials, private storage and recovery controls. No system can promise absolute security. Users should protect their accounts and report suspected unauthorised access through Chris Drummond at the contact above, marked “ServiceOS security”.

Some suppliers or their support teams may process information outside the UK. Production Supabase database storage and the independent AWS backup destination are in London; TEST Supabase is in Ireland. Supplier support and other services can involve additional countries. The final supplier schedule must identify the applicable UK transfer safeguard for each relevant flow before launch. Copies of the applicable safeguards can be requested from our privacy contact. London backup storage does not establish UK-only processing.

8. Retention and deletion

We determine retention according to the purpose of each category, operational need and applicable legal requirements. Workspace history, financial records, recordings, access logs and account information need separate schedules.

Disconnecting a source, deleting an original record and deleting its retained ServiceOS copies are different actions. A deletion request may also need to cover historical versions and derived records. We assess requests and explain any lawful reason for retaining particular information.

Backups can have separate expiry and deletion-protection periods. Copies awaiting disposal remain restricted to recovery and authorised purposes. Applicable deletion instructions must be reapplied if a backup is restored.

Proposed pilot retention for approval: review retained copies at the end of the 90-day pilot. Keep records longer only where necessary for an identified continuing service, open job/dispute, or legal obligation, and record the reason and next review date. Source-system accounting records retain their separate retention requirements.

Proposed exit targets: provide a 30-day export opportunity after termination, remove active copies within 30 days after return/deletion is agreed, and remove recovery copies within 90 days after active deletion, subject to documented legal exceptions. Current backups have minimum locks and some have no automatic expiry. These targets must be approved and verified across source versions, derived records and recovery copies before publication. This draft does not delete data.

9. Your rights

Depending on the processing and applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal information. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may object to direct marketing at any time. Contact us using the details above. We may need to verify your identity; not every right applies in every circumstance.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office or another relevant supervisory authority where applicable. You do not need to contact us first, although we welcome the opportunity to resolve concerns.

10. Website technologies and changes

ServiceOS stores its sign-in session in browser local storage and refreshes it while authorised. It also uses local storage for selected workspace/connection preferences and saved operator action drafts, session storage for a selected call, and a sidebar preference cookie with a seven-day lifetime. Local-storage items can persist until removed or browser data is cleared; closing a tab does not necessarily remove them. Use a trusted device and sign out when finished.

The existing application requests Google-hosted fonts. Hosting/security services may process request logs. No optional analytics SDK was identified in the inspected application source, but this is not a completed network/cookie audit of every enabled module. The final cookie inventory and any required choices must match the deployed behaviour before publication.

We will date updates and provide appropriate notice of material changes. Updating this notice does not itself authorise a new purpose for personal information.
