Drummonds — OpenFolk ServiceOS end-user terms

Draft for review · 13 October 2026 · Not published, accepted or effective. These are proposed terms for authorised users of Drummonds’ deployment, not OpenFolk’s commercial terms for every client. Read with the publication review.

1. About this deployment

DRUMMOND HEATING LTD., company number 07556814, makes its ServiceOS workspace available for its authorised business purposes. Registered office: Unit 15 Westlink Belbins Business Park, Cupernham Lane, Romsey, Hampshire, England, SO51 7JF. In these terms, “Drummonds”, “we” and “us” mean that company.

Contact Chris Drummond, chris@drummondheating.co.uk, marked “ServiceOS support”, or write to our registered office. Chris is the nominated contact for support, privacy and security enquiries about this deployment. Effective date: to be set on approval and publication.

This is Drummonds’ internal application, developed and supported by Chris Drummond, who supplies the software personally and works directly with Drummonds. OpenFolk ServiceOS is the software name, not a separate contracting company identified by these terms. These terms and the use of Drummonds’ Intuit developer workspace do not transfer ownership of ServiceOS or its reusable components.

These terms govern access by our authorised staff, contractors and other expressly approved users. They do not replace employment terms, customer heating-service contracts or any separately agreed development or software-licensing arrangements. Drummonds must adopt these terms for authorised users and confirm its underlying software rights before publication. A separate pilot agreement records the arrangement between Chris and Drummonds; these user terms do not replace it.

2. Permitted use and access

Subject to the applicable software rights, we permit you to use the enabled workspace features for Drummonds’ authorised work while your access remains active. Access is personal, role-dependent and must not be transferred or shared.

Protect your sign-in details, follow our security instructions and promptly report suspected compromise. Do not bypass permissions, access another organisation’s data, disrupt the service, upload unlawful material or connect an account you lack authority to connect. Any restriction on reverse engineering applies only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Our administrators may change or withdraw access as responsibilities change or where necessary to protect the service. Where practicable, they will explain a material restriction and the route to resolve it. Access to information is not permission to disclose it outside the authorised work.

3. Connected information

Only designated administrators may authorise a data source or change capture scope. Connections may bring in agreed historical records as well as new activity. Notices, permissions and applicable legal requirements must be addressed before capturing communications or using information for a new purpose.

Coverage depends on source permissions, retention, rate limits, configuration and availability. “Connected” does not mean that every record is imported, correctly matched or fully analysed. Check coverage, dates and source evidence when relying on a summary. The original business systems remain relevant records of authority.

Source subscriptions and their terms remain separate. Changes at a provider may require reauthorisation or prevent a feature from working. A roadmap or demo is not a promise that a feature is enabled or suitable for an operational decision.

4. QuickBooks Online

An authorised QuickBooks administrator must approve the intended company through Intuit. OpenFolk ServiceOS is independent of Intuit. It does not replace QuickBooks, accounting advice, statutory recordkeeping or financial review.

The initial connector is designed to read agreed accounting records and does not create, edit or delete transactions or initiate payments. Intuit’s Accounting permission includes read and write capabilities; the implementation restricts its use to reads. Separate payment-processing and payroll functions are outside this initial connection. A future write feature requires a separately defined and authorised scope.

An authorised administrator may revoke Intuit access or request disconnection through our support contact. Disconnection does not delete QuickBooks records or automatically erase ServiceOS history. Reconnection, including migration to an OpenFolk-owned developer app, may require fresh authorisation.

The sandbox connection and imports are verified. The live Drummonds company remains unconnected at the draft date; live availability is subject to release verification. QuickBooks data must not be used for model training or fine-tuning.

5. AI and business judgement

Summaries, suggested actions, linked timelines and financial indicators can be wrong or incomplete. Verify important findings against source records. Financial views need their currency, period, accounting basis and coverage to be understood; payments, invoices and credits must not simply be added together as revenue.

Outputs are not an audit, tax filing, investment recommendation or guarantee of revenue, profit or savings. Any enabled scorecards require clear measures, appropriate workforce information and a route to correct disputed evidence. Meaningful human review is required before decisions about pay, employment or other significant outcomes. These terms do not authorise undisclosed monitoring or automatically changing someone’s pay.

Only authorised budget holders may enable paid AI, telephony or other chargeable usage within an agreed budget. A trial or demo does not authorise unlimited spending.

6. Confidentiality, support and privacy

Keep workspace information confidential and use it only for authorised purposes. OpenFolk’s authorised support operators may examine relevant Drummonds information to resolve problems and improve our implementation under the agreed instructions, permissions and confidentiality duties. Their access to other clients does not permit sharing one client’s underlying information with another.

The deployment privacy notice explains personal-information handling. Drummonds must document its authorised technical administration and its arrangements with external providers, including security, international processing, incidents, rights requests and return/deletion. Where a provider acts as a processor, the appropriate processing agreement is required. User acceptance of these terms does not replace those arrangements or provide blanket consent for employee or correspondent data processing.

Proposed deployment commitment to adopt and verify: no sale of identifiable workspace data, general-purpose model training or unrelated-client reuse. Support and improvements for Drummonds remain within the agreed purpose and instructions.

7. Service availability and records

Any service levels, support commitments and backup obligations need to be recorded in the agreed operational arrangements. These terms do not guarantee availability, response times or recovery periods. Material stale, missing or unverified information should be identified rather than presented as successfully processed.

Do not treat a demo, local test or restored historical backup as evidence that current live capture or full recovery works. Keep required records in approved systems and follow the organisation’s retention instructions.

8. Ending access, exports and deletion

When access ends, stop using the workspace and return or protect information as instructed. Authorised administrators may request exports or deletion through the support contact above. Individual rights requests follow the privacy notice.

The proposed pilot agreement sets a 30-day export opportunity after termination, active-data deletion within 30 days after return/deletion is agreed, and recovery-copy removal within 90 days after active deletion, with lawful exceptions. Those targets require approval and verification before these terms become effective. Revoking a connection is not the same as deleting stored history. Historical versions and derived records require explicit handling. Retained recovery copies must remain restricted, and deletion instructions must be reapplied after restoration.

No immediate or automatic 30-day deletion promise is made by this draft. It does not authorise changing any existing retained records or backup configuration.

9. Responsibility and disputes

Users remain responsible for acting within their authority and checking material outputs. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including applicable liability for fraud, fraudulent misrepresentation or death or personal injury caused by negligence.

No new user indemnity or monetary liability cap is imposed by these terms. Existing employment and supplier agreements remain separate. These internal access terms do not set commercial fees, renewal obligations or liabilities for any separate development arrangement.

England and Wales law applies and its courts have jurisdiction, subject to mandatory applicable law. Raise concerns through the support contact above. This does not prevent urgent relief, complaints to a competent authority or applicable statutory rights.

Drummonds will communicate material changes to authorised users through their registered business email, normally at least 14 days before they take effect. Urgent legal or security changes may take effect sooner with an explanation. Where a change requires agreement, Drummonds will obtain it expressly; a notice alone is not acceptance. A terms update does not retrospectively expand data-use permissions or change software ownership.
