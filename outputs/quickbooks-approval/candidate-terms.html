Drummonds — ServiceOS authorised-user terms

Publication candidate prepared 13 October 2026. Not effective until adopted and published.

This workspace

DRUMMOND HEATING LTD., company number 07556814, makes its ServiceOS workspace available for authorised business use. Registered office: Unit 15 Westlink Belbins Business Park, Cupernham Lane, Romsey, Hampshire, England, SO51 7JF. Contact Chris Drummond at chris@drummondheating.co.uk, marked “ServiceOS support”.

Chris supplies and supports the software personally. OpenFolk ServiceOS is the software name, not a separate contracting company identified by these terms. These terms do not transfer software ownership, set subscription fees or replace employment, heating-service, development or separately agreed pilot arrangements. They apply to expressly authorised staff, contractors and other users of Drummonds’ deployment.

Access and permitted use

Use enabled features only for authorised Drummonds work and while your access remains active. Access is personal, role-dependent and must not be shared. Protect your sign-in details and report suspected compromise promptly.

Do not bypass permissions, access another organisation’s information, disrupt the service, upload unlawful material or connect an account without authority. Any restriction on reverse engineering applies only to the extent permitted by law. Administrators may change or withdraw access as responsibilities change or to protect the service; where practicable they will explain a material restriction and how to resolve it.

Connected records

Only designated administrators may authorise a source or change capture scope. Historical and new records may be imported within the authorised scope. Appropriate notices, permissions and legal requirements must be addressed before capturing communications or using information for a new purpose.

Coverage depends on source permissions, retention, rate limits and availability. A connected status does not mean every record is captured, correctly matched or analysed. Check dates, coverage and underlying evidence. Source subscriptions and terms remain separate, and provider changes may require reauthorisation. A roadmap or demonstration does not establish that a feature is operational.

QuickBooks

An authorised QuickBooks administrator must approve the intended company through Intuit. OpenFolk ServiceOS is independent of Intuit and does not replace QuickBooks, accounting advice or statutory records.

Although Intuit’s Accounting permission includes writes, the initial ServiceOS connector reads agreed records only. It does not create, edit or delete accounting transactions or move money. Separate payment-processing and payroll functions are excluded. A future write feature requires a separately authorised scope.

Financial views are limited to Drummonds owners/admins and explicitly authorised, logged support operators. QuickBooks information must not be used for model training or fine-tuning. The current connector does not send those records to an AI processing pipeline.

An authorised administrator can revoke access through Intuit or arrange disconnection through our support contact. Disconnection preserves source records and does not automatically erase imported history. Reconnection or migration to another developer app may require fresh company authorisation.

Judgement and confidentiality

Summaries, suggested actions, timelines and indicators may be wrong or incomplete. Verify material findings against source evidence. Understand the currency, period, accounting basis and coverage of financial views; do not simply add payments, invoices and credits together as revenue. Outputs are not an audit, tax filing, investment recommendation or guarantee of financial results.

Significant decisions about pay or employment require meaningful human review. Scorecards need clear measures, appropriate workforce information and a correction process. These terms do not authorise undisclosed monitoring or automatic pay changes. Only authorised budget holders may approve chargeable usage within an agreed budget.

Keep workspace information confidential. Permitted support operators may access relevant information under Drummonds’ instructions to resolve problems and improve its implementation. Information must not be sold, used for general-purpose model training or reused for unrelated clients. The deployment privacy notice describes handling; accepting these terms is not blanket consent for employee or correspondent processing.

Service, exit and records

Service levels and recovery obligations require separate agreement. These terms do not guarantee uptime, response times or recovery periods. Missing, stale or unverified information must not be represented as successfully processed. Keep required records in approved systems.

When access ends, stop using the workspace and return or protect information as instructed. Administrators can request exports or deletion through Chris. The pilot exit targets are a 30-day export opportunity after termination, active-copy removal within 30 days after return/deletion is agreed, and recovery-copy removal within 90 days after active deletion, subject to documented legal exceptions.

These are support-led targets, not a statement that automatic expiry or whole-workspace export is already operational. Confirm the scope and completion arrangements for each request. Historical versions, derived records and backup locks require explicit handling. Deletion instructions must be reapplied before restored data returns to use. Statutory rights and any separately agreed binding commitments are unaffected.

Responsibility and changes

Act within your authority and check material outputs. Nothing excludes rights or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including applicable liability for fraud, fraudulent misrepresentation or death or personal injury caused by negligence. These terms impose no new user indemnity or monetary liability cap.

England and Wales law applies, with its courts having jurisdiction subject to mandatory applicable law. Raise concerns through the support contact; this does not prevent urgent relief or complaints to a competent authority.

Drummonds will communicate material changes through registered business email, normally at least 14 days in advance. Urgent legal or security changes may take effect sooner with an explanation. Where agreement is required, it must be obtained expressly; notice alone is not acceptance. An update does not retrospectively expand data-use permission or change ownership.
