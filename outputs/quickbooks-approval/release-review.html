Reference basis and limits

These are original ServiceOS drafts based on the requested product scope and repository evidence. They are not copied Intuit terms. The current Intuit portal requires public EULA/privacy links and an app assessment before production keys are released. Review Intuit's Developer Terms alongside the account's actual applicable agreement; do not accept new terms as part of drafting these documents.

The ICO privacy-information checklist informed the review fields for identity, sources, purposes, bases, recipients, retention, transfers and rights. Its page says guidance is under review following the Data (Use and Access) Act; check current guidance before final adoption.

The ICO storage-limitation guidance supports defining retention by purpose instead of retaining personal information indefinitely by default. The ICO controller/processor guidance informs review of actual processor arrangements. The ICO transfer guide informs the need to verify locations, overseas access and safeguards. These references do not validate OpenFolk's current contracts or technical configuration.
