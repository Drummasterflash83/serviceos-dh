## Measurement rules for every contract

Each eventual measurement must retain metric definition/version, source record
list, tenant/provider/account/environment, period/timezone, numerator/denominator,
freshness, exclusions, correction history and uncertainty. Targets and observation
windows must be agreed **before** intervention evaluation. Unknowns are not zero.
Cost savings, released capacity, additional revenue, collections and profit are
different outcomes; overlapping opportunities must not be summed.

Integrity conditions already required by the build contract are zero cross-tenant
disclosure, zero silent source mutation, zero invented monetary/date/completion
facts and no duplicate side-effects in tested replay. These are invariants, not
an invented universal coverage/accuracy threshold. Coverage and latency thresholds
must be agreed for the actual source and capability. No blanket 95% target is set.
