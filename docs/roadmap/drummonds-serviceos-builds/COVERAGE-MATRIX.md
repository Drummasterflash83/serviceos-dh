This matrix prevents a workflow disappearing between LEARN and operational delivery.
It maps capabilities, not a promise to reproduce every Perplexity implementation.

## Related needs and constraints

- Intake and missed-call promises use existing communications/LEARN, not a second
  receptionist build. Map their observed consequences in 08 and prioritise through 09.
- Tender triage/scoping/outcomes use 04 policy, 05 opportunity evidence and 12/18
  common quote/pack capabilities. Tender contribution and further-works GP are not
  interchangeable. No speculative tender acquisition platform is commissioned.
- Supplier bills, purchase orders, returns and late costs connect 06, 11, 14 and 17.
- Contracts need field-level/source precedence and effective dates. Financial truth
  remains in accounting evidence; operational job-code truth remains Commusoft for
  Drummonds. Canonical links never erase original identifiers.
- Customer commitments and accepted-to-booked communication are explicit in 05,
  08, 12, 13 and 19; completion of a quote is not the end of the relationship.
- Local AI is evaluated in 07 and reused; no accounting model training or silent
  provider fallback. Cost reduction is measured from billing and effort evidence,
  not inferred from reported credit counts.
- Historical New Dawn target conflicts, ownership gaps and unavailable scripts are
  resolved through 00/02/04; they are never silently replaced with model guesses.
- Individual/group Slack DMs are excluded. Authorised channel history and replies
  remain in scope with visibility inherited by every derivative.
- Preserve existing core, current client UX and Drummonds branding throughout.
  Operator access is explicit/audited; ordinary client isolation remains enforced.
- Every stage has a second-tenant reuse check; 23 packages the accumulated proof.
  This is reusable engineering, not permission to share private company learning.
