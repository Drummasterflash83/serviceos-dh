# M1 job Home: customer work first

The first screen in the audited MD presentation leads with On-hold or Pending jobs whose customer **and** site have confirmed, source-versioned links. It shows those customer groups and their jobs immediately after the hero. Each job opens the existing audited exact-source detail. The full paged Commusoft list remains available through a separate “Browse every recorded open job” action or a status button; opening it is when its first page is read.

The headline is the count of confirmed linked customer jobs in those statuses. Saved On-hold, Pending and Open totals cover the wider recorded workload, including office work and jobs whose identity is still under review. Neither status nor creation age proves urgency, a hold cause, an owner, or time in the current stage. If the confirmed-link read fails, the page says so and offers the full recorded list. A failed refresh retains only the dated last-good result for the same actor, tenant and access reason.

The client-facing tiles use short language: dated job status check, confirmed customer groups, and unavailable visit/ownership evidence. Source coverage arithmetic and score policy detail stay in the OpenFolk operator disclosure. No numeric job, customer or team score is published. The page adds no link, provider write, access grant, or new database read function.

## Signed-in acceptance check

On desktop and phone, use the short-lived audited `/client-md` presentation with a fresh access reason:

1. Home starts with “Customer jobs to check”; its number agrees with the visible confirmed group jobs and links open the correct saved job.
2. The jobs have clear On-hold or Pending labels, a recorded source date, and no invented urgency, owner or supplier-wait clock.
3. Jobs, Customers and Team tiles follow the confirmed work. Team explicitly says visit data is awaited; no numeric score appears.
4. “Browse every recorded open job” reveals status filters, exact rows and paging; a status shortcut opens the corresponding filter. The initial Home does not request the full first page.
5. Refresh, navigation away/back and a narrow phone viewport preserve honest dated data and an actionable error if a read fails. The OpenFolk access banner remains small after the reason is entered; ordinary Drummonds login remains disabled pending onboarding.

The signed-in check is release evidence, not Drummonds staff acceptance. Record desktop/phone observations and any missing or stale data in the private handback. Do not copy private customer names or counts into public Git.
