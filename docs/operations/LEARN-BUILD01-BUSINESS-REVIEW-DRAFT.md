## What Chris should see in LIVE

1. **Job attention:** Open LEARN → Jobs → Captured Commusoft jobs. A recorded
   on-hold job appears in the hold filter; a job whose customer relationship
   needs review appears in the source-review filter. Each shows its recorded
   Commusoft status and observation time. The first audited OpenFolk review
   task goes to the confirmed Owner / MD login and changes state only after
   that person accepts it. This task is an OpenFolk handoff, not a claimed
   Commusoft technician appointment.
2. **Representative source evidence:** Inspect a reviewed direct-customer job,
   a reviewed site-to-customer job, a source-backed job still awaiting identity
   review, a missing-parent job, and a recorded-complete job. The first two
   reviewed paths matched fresh read-only Commusoft GETs. Missing parents stay
   flagged, and recorded completion has no invented time-zone or elapsed-time
   score. Job codes remain strings, including leading zeros; no QuickBooks
   work-code crosswalk is inferred.
3. **Communications:** Phone, Email and saved public Slack channel evidence can
   be opened from LEARN. Show the original Slack message and its saved source
   context; current public-channel parent/reply capture is checked separately
   from historical totals. Individual and group Slack DMs remain excluded.
4. **Cards and scoring:** Customer cards show retained evidence and truthful
   timestamps. Job progress/completion is the first scoring priority, but no
   numerical job or employee score is published until source freshness,
   completion meaning, eligible population and Chris-approved criteria are
   established. Review-required records are not silently scored as healthy.

## Limits to accept explicitly

- The recorded LIVE open/pending/on-hold sample has **295 jobs**: ten reviewed
  job-card links, 255 with retained customer/site source but unreviewed
  customer identity, 29 missing a parent source and one awaiting job-link
  review. Chris chose a representative Build 01 review; the remaining cases
  must stay visible, unlinked and unscored for continued review.
- All **160** original approved public Slack channels are archived. Direct
  provider reads found no post-cutoff parent in any of them and no post-cutoff
  reply in all **870** known earlier thread roots. Their subscriptions and
  fallback windows remain visible; empty windows are not described as message
  deliveries. The separate **20 active public channels** have real signed
  parent and reply capture. Private channels and DMs are not included in this
  acceptance sample.
- Historical Email intake is capped at three years for the full import while
  already retained older messages stay intact. Current mailbox capture and
  historical catch-up are assessed separately. Any remaining source-to-card
  identity review stays visible; no ambiguous company is inferred.
- Full recovery must prove the replacement database/Auth/frontend/current
  Edge source and required runtime credentials can restart the application
  and resume bounded provider capture. A successful database backup alone is
  insufficient. The local communication-file backup depends on the Mac being
  awake and is not an always-on cloud schedule.
