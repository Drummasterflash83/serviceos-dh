# Job-first MD Home and reliable paged work · 21 October 2026

The MD Home currently calls every confirmed On-hold/Pending record “customer work” but does not show what Commusoft says the work is. A bounded LIVE review found office and commercial record descriptions among those links. A held status by itself is not evidence that field work is urgent. On a narrow signed-in screen, the large Money panel also pushes the linked job rows below the first viewport. The paged work list also times out against the full retained history, even after the Home totals and customer groups became current. This outcome shows the saved source description, puts a concise Jobs/Customers/Team overview directly after the lead action, then the linked jobs, makes the later Home money view compact, and repairs the full work-list reader. Only the first confirmed customer group starts expanded, so the full list remains reachable without opening every group on a phone. Source-customer matching and daily roster coverage checks are shown and fetched only in the OpenFolk operator view; the MD presentation and future client view use dated job totals and retain every open job through the full paged work list.

## Contract and scope

- The input is the **current retained Commusoft job source version** already selected by `new_dawn_commusoft_current_job_source`. There is no new provider call. `description` is trimmed, capped at 160 characters, and emitted as `null` if absent. The UI renders it as text and labels a missing description explicitly. Neither the SQL nor the UI classifies field/office work or urgency.
- The response advances to `new-dawn-customer-blockers/2`; the client accepts v1 during rollout and tells the user to open the job for its description until v2 is installed. In v2, an absent source description is labelled as such. The client validates the added key, length and exact source identity. Tenant, actor, audit, customer/site matching, date and row limits remain enforced.
- The lead Home count includes **all** saved On-hold and Pending jobs. Separate counted actions open each status in the full paged work list, including jobs without a confirmed customer match; the Customers tile opens confirmed customer/site groups. Those groups call their records customer jobs to check, without implying urgency or a field visit. Unlinked jobs remain in the paged list and operator identity-review queue. This change does not create links, assign staff, change statuses, change scores or enable a client login.
- The Jobs/Customers/Team tiles now appear immediately after the job lead action, followed by linked customer work, status buckets, the click-to-load oldest On-hold route and the full paged open-job browser, all before company Money. They give real saved counts and actionable paths in short language. Team names the missing diary evidence; no numeric score is fabricated. The Home Money strip keeps dated QuickBooks accounts GP, MD-only NP and credit-inclusive A/R; unsupported Further Works and recurring-revenue metrics remain explained in the full Money view. Money dates use DD/MM/YYYY and exact GBP decimals show `£` without conversion to floating point. The saved reporting period, basis, source time and older-period warning stay visible. No accounting values or report requests change.
- The Jobs tile reuses LEARN's existing audited provider-newest comparison and labels its actual check time. It explicitly says that older jobs and later updates may still be missing. A newly opened Home is never itself evidence that Commusoft intake is current; a failed or unavailable provider comparison stays visible as unavailable.
- The Jobs tile also shows the existing exact current-job link count and the number still awaiting matching from the audited status summary. That count is a job-source match, not proof that every customer and site is confirmed; the separate Customers tile uses the stricter customer/site group reader.
- Client-facing Home copy uses plain job and customer language, while retaining checked-at times, the explicit “on record” boundary and unconfirmed-match cues. Detailed Commusoft provenance stays in the job detail disclosure and OpenFolk operator controls. The Jobs tile calls its identity count “job matches checked,” because that count alone does not certify both customer and site identities.
- The full Money view shows the verified period's net margin without treating the older New Dawn 8% aim or £58,000 Further Works figure as an approved current target. It asks for a confirmed target before a comparison; the underlying historical brief remains in the review pack.
- This release depends on `20261113000000` and `20261113010000` from the reliable current-source Home release. It must not install before both are verified on LIVE.
- The existing audited `new_dawn_open_job_page` keeps its actor, tenant, flag, source snapshot, keyset cursor, status filter, source context, identity and audit contracts. Its base reader uses the exact private current-source projection for keys unchanged since the cursor anchor. Only keys whose projection changed after the anchor scan their retained history to reconstruct the old page. Ambiguous equal-time versions remain withheld. No source row, link, grant or client flag changes. The source projection has no direct client grant.

## Evidence and acceptance

In disposable PostgreSQL 17, the final guarded description migration returned a v2 customer-group row with synthetic description “For Invoicing,” and the exact rollback restored the prior function hash. The page-reader migration passed synthetic first/second/third pages, status filter, wrong-tenant and bad-cursor denial, equal-time conflict withholding, client flag denial, operator audit and a later-import snapshot test. Its exact rollback restored the prior hash and reapplication passed. The read-only LIVE preflight found the exact prior function and already installed dependency versions. A bounded read-only production query over the projected current jobs completed in 123 ms; this is a query component, not a full page RPC performance claim. Parser tests cover v1 compatibility, the v2 description and malformed/missing v2 fields. Release-script tests cover exact dependencies, grants, version and function hash. Focused Money tests cover exact large and negative GBP amounts and UK dates. These are preparation checks, not a LIVE release of this PR.

The reproducible load fixture `scripts/verify/new-dawn-open-job-page-current-source-load.sql` runs after `node scripts/verify/new-dawn-open-job-page-current-source-synthetic.mjs` in a disposable PostgreSQL 17 database. It adds 15,000 synthetic job keys and eight synthetic observations per key, then calls the exact first, second and On-hold pages at size 40. The verified isolated run had 15,004 projected keys and 120,008 observations including the small base fixture; timings were 11.7 ms, 9.1 ms and 9.0 ms respectively. This detects a gross history-scan regression at source-scale volume, but local hardware, synthetic status distribution and stubbed customer/site lookup differ from LIVE. Signed-in production RPC timing remains required after installation.
