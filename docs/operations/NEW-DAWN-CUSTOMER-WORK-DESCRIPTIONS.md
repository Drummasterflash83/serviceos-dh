# Customer-linked work descriptions · 21 October 2026

The MD Home currently calls every confirmed On-hold/Pending record “customer work” but does not show what Commusoft says the work is. A bounded LIVE review found office and commercial record descriptions among those links. A held status by itself is not evidence that field work is urgent. On a narrow signed-in screen, the large Money panel also pushes the linked job rows below the first viewport. This outcome shows the saved source description, puts a concise Jobs/Customers/Team overview directly after the lead action, then the linked jobs, and makes the later Home money view compact.

## Contract and scope

- The input is the **current retained Commusoft job source version** already selected by `new_dawn_commusoft_current_job_source`. There is no new provider call. `description` is trimmed, capped at 160 characters, and emitted as `null` if absent. The UI renders it as text and labels a missing description explicitly. Neither the SQL nor the UI classifies field/office work or urgency.
- The response advances to `new-dawn-customer-blockers/2`; the client accepts v1 during rollout and tells the user to open the job for its description until v2 is installed. In v2, an absent source description is labelled as such. The client validates the added key, length and exact source identity. Tenant, actor, audit, customer/site matching, date and row limits remain enforced.
- The MD Home describes confirmed records as linked records requiring inspection. The unlinked On-hold/Pending jobs remain in the paged list and operator identity-review queue. This change does not create links, assign staff, change statuses, change scores or enable a client login.
- The Jobs/Customers/Team tiles now appear immediately after the job lead action, before the linked customer job list and company Money. They give real saved counts and actionable paths in short language. Team names the missing diary evidence; no numeric score is fabricated. The Home Money strip keeps dated QuickBooks accounts GP, MD-only NP and credit-inclusive A/R; unsupported Further Works and recurring-revenue metrics remain explained in the full Money view. Money dates use DD/MM/YYYY and exact GBP decimals show `£` without conversion to floating point. The saved reporting period, basis, source time and older-period warning stay visible. No accounting values or report requests change.
- This release depends on `20261113000000` and `20261113010000` from the reliable current-source Home release. It must not install before both are verified on LIVE.
