## Information and sources

We bring together information from our customers, suppliers, staff and authorised business systems. This may include names, contact details, addresses, customer accounts, sites, jobs, appointments, operational notes, emails, call records, recordings, transcripts, voicemails, authorised Slack channel messages and replies, accounting records, access logs and derived summaries.

Connected sources include Google Workspace, our phone provider, Slack, Commusoft and, when authorised, QuickBooks Online. Capture depends on the enabled features, source permissions and agreed scope. Our current Slack intake excludes individual and group direct messages. A connection does not establish complete or current coverage.

## Why we use information

We use information to deliver heating services, coordinate work, respond to customers, follow up outstanding tasks, understand invoices and payments, manage our operations, secure the workspace and resolve service problems.

Our bases depend on the purpose and individual concerned:

- Performance of a contract where needed to deliver services contracted directly with an individual.
- Legitimate interests in coordinating business-customer work, supplier relationships, communications and service delivery, and in protecting our information and systems.
- Legal obligations where particular financial or other records must be kept by law.
- Consent where required for optional processing and actually obtained.

A customer contract is not the basis for every correspondent or employee. Any use of special-category or criminal-offence information requires a separate applicable condition. Necessary customer and job details enable us to provide services; account information enables workspace access. We explain specific collection requirements where applicable. Declining a connection limits the information available in ServiceOS.

## QuickBooks Online

An authorised QuickBooks administrator chooses the company and grants access through Intuit. ServiceOS uses tokens rather than that person’s QuickBooks password. It reads agreed accounting contacts and transactions to place invoices, payments, balances and related records alongside customer and job information.

Intuit’s Accounting permission includes read and write capability. Our initial connector restricts its implementation to reads: it does not create, edit or delete accounting transactions or initiate payments. The separate payments API and payroll are outside this connection.

Financial views are limited to Drummonds company owners/admins and explicitly authorised, logged OpenFolk support operators. ServiceOS roles are administered separately from QuickBooks roles. **QuickBooks information is excluded from model training and fine-tuning. The current connector does not send it to an AI processing pipeline.**

An authorised administrator can revoke access through Intuit, or use the ServiceOS support contact to arrange disconnection. Disconnection does not change the source records in QuickBooks or automatically erase retained ServiceOS history.

## AI and human decisions

Enabled AI and speech features elsewhere in ServiceOS may process relevant communications to produce summaries, transcripts or suggested actions. Outputs can be wrong, incomplete or linked incorrectly and must be checked against source evidence.

Any workforce indicators require clear purposes, measures, coverage and correction procedures. Significant decisions about pay or employment require meaningful human review. This notice does not authorise undisclosed monitoring or automatic pay changes. Identifiable workspace information must not be sold, used for general-purpose model training or reused for unrelated clients. Authorised Drummonds-specific support and improvement remain within the agreed purposes.

## Access and service providers

Authorised Drummonds users receive role-based access. Permitted OpenFolk support operators may inspect relevant Drummonds information through explicit client selection and logged actions. Access to several clients does not permit sharing one client’s underlying information with another.

Our technical services include Supabase for authentication, database, private storage and server functions; Vercel for frontend hosting and web requests; AWS for approved independent recovery storage; Cloudflare for domain/DNS services; and OpenAI and Vapi for enabled AI/voice features. The current QuickBooks connector sends no accounting records to OpenAI or Vapi. Google receives network request information when the application loads its hosted fonts. Connected providers also operate under their own terms and privacy notices.

We may share relevant information with professional advisers subject to appropriate duties, where legally required, or for a lawful business transfer with safeguards.

## Security and international processing

We use access restrictions, protected server-side credentials and private storage. No system offers absolute security. Report suspected unauthorised access to Chris, marked “ServiceOS security”.

The production Supabase database and independent AWS recovery destination are in London; TEST Supabase is in Ireland. Supplier infrastructure or support can involve other countries. These storage locations do not establish UK-only processing. Where UK transfer rules apply, Drummonds must use an applicable adequacy arrangement or appropriate contractual safeguards and any required additional measures. Contact Chris for details and copies of the safeguards applicable to a particular flow. This notice does not certify supplier arrangements that have not been reviewed.

## Retention, return and deletion

We decide retention by category and purpose, considering ongoing services, open jobs, disputes, security needs and legal obligations. We review retained pilot information at the end of the 90-day pilot and record a reason and next review date where continued retention is needed. Original source-system accounting records have separate retention requirements.

Our pilot exit targets are a **30-day export opportunity after termination**, removal of active copies **within 30 days after return/deletion is agreed**, and removal of recovery copies **within 90 days after active deletion**, subject to documented legal exceptions. These are targets for a support-led process; they are not a claim that automatic deletion or whole-workspace export is already implemented. We confirm the requested scope, delivery arrangements, applicable locks and achievable completion dates with the requester.

Deleting a source record or disconnecting a source is different from deleting retained versions, derived information and recovery copies. Existing backups have minimum deletion-protection periods and some have no automatic expiry. Recovery copies remain restricted; applicable deletion instructions must be reapplied before restored information is put back into use. Contact Chris to request return, deletion or an explanation of continued retention. These operational targets do not limit statutory rights or applicable legal response deadlines.

## Your rights

Depending on the processing and applicable law, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. We may need to verify identity, and not every right applies in every circumstance.

**You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may object to direct marketing at any time.**

You may complain to the [UK Information Commissioner’s Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/). You do not need to contact us first.

## Website storage and updates

ServiceOS keeps sign-in sessions and selected workspace/connection preferences in browser local storage, a selected call in session storage, and a sidebar preference cookie for seven days. Local storage can remain until removed; closing a tab does not necessarily remove it. Use a trusted device and sign out afterwards. The app loads Google-hosted fonts, and hosting/security services may keep request logs. Any additional optional processing requires appropriate information and choices.

We date material updates and provide appropriate notice. Changing this notice does not itself authorise a new processing purpose.
