## 2. Permitted use and access

Subject to the applicable supplier licence, we permit you to use the enabled
workspace features for Drummonds’ authorised work while your access remains active.
Access is personal, role-dependent and must not be transferred or shared.

Protect your sign-in details, follow our security instructions and promptly report
suspected compromise. Do not bypass permissions, access another organisation’s
data, disrupt the service, upload unlawful material or connect an account you lack
authority to connect. Any restriction on reverse engineering applies only to the
extent permitted by applicable law.

Our administrators may change or withdraw access as responsibilities change or
where necessary to protect the service. Where practicable, they will explain a
material restriction and the route to resolve it. Access to information is not
permission to disclose it outside the authorised work.

## 3. Connected information

Only designated administrators may authorise a data source or change capture
scope. Connections may bring in agreed historical records as well as new activity.
Notices, permissions and applicable legal requirements must be addressed before
capturing communications or using information for a new purpose.

Coverage depends on source permissions, retention, rate limits, configuration and
availability. “Connected” does not mean that every record is imported, correctly
matched or fully analysed. Check coverage, dates and source evidence when relying
on a summary. The original business systems remain relevant records of authority.

Source subscriptions and their terms remain separate. Changes at a provider may
require reauthorisation or prevent a feature from working. A roadmap or demo is
not a promise that a feature is enabled or suitable for an operational decision.

## 4. QuickBooks Online

An authorised QuickBooks administrator must approve the intended company through
Intuit. OpenFolk ServiceOS is independent of Intuit. It does not replace QuickBooks,
accounting advice, statutory recordkeeping or financial review.

The initial connector is designed to read agreed accounting records and does not
create, edit or delete transactions or initiate payments. Intuit’s Accounting
permission includes read and write capabilities; the implementation restricts its
use to reads. Separate payment-processing and payroll functions are outside this
initial connection. A future write feature requires a separately defined and
authorised scope.

An authorised administrator may revoke Intuit access or request disconnection
through our support contact. Disconnection does not delete QuickBooks records or
automatically erase ServiceOS history. Reconnection, including migration to an
OpenFolk-owned developer app, may require fresh authorisation.

## 5. AI and business judgement

Summaries, suggested actions, linked timelines and financial indicators can be
wrong or incomplete. Verify important findings against source records. Financial
views need their currency, period, accounting basis and coverage to be understood;
payments, invoices and credits must not simply be added together as revenue.

Outputs are not an audit, tax filing, investment recommendation or guarantee of
revenue, profit or savings. Any enabled scorecards require clear measures,
appropriate workforce information and a route to correct disputed evidence.
Meaningful human review is required before decisions about pay, employment or
other significant outcomes. These terms do not authorise undisclosed monitoring
or automatically changing someone’s pay.

Only authorised budget holders may enable paid AI, telephony or other chargeable
usage within an agreed budget. A trial or demo does not authorise unlimited spending.

agreement or provide blanket consent for employee or correspondent data processing.

**Proposed supplier commitment to adopt and verify:** no sale of identifiable
